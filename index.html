Section 01 | Deep Time Eons

The Precambrian Era (~4.6 Ga – 541 Ma)

Location: Hadean Volcanic Crust to Proterozoic Primordial Oceans

Milestone #1 • ~3.8 Ga Prokaryotic Origin

Origin of Single-Celled Prokaryotic Life

"Stepping onto the boiling shoreline, atmospheric sensors register zero free oxygen—predominantly methane, ammonia, and carbon dioxide. Deep in submarine hydrothermal vents, simple lipid membranes encapsulated self-replicating genetic material, forming the first unicellular prokaryotic microbes."

Milestone #2 • ~2.4 Ga Atmospheric Transformation

The Great Oxidation Event (GOE)

"A revolutionary metabolic breakthrough has occurred! Microscopic cyanobacteria evolved oxygenic photosynthesis. Over centuries, oxygen saturated chemical sinks in ocean iron deposits and flooded the atmosphere, transforming Earth's climate and sky forever."

⚡ Evolutionary Cause-and-Effect #1

Photosynthetic Activity → Atmospheric Oxygen Shift → Aerobic Radiation: The continuous production of photosynthetic oxygen wiped out ancient obligate anaerobic prokaryotes in a massive die-off, but fundamentally created the high-energy oxidative atmosphere required for complex eukaryotic cells and multicellular organismal body plans.

"The ocean floor is teeming with alien anatomical architectures! In a stunningly rapid evolutionary burst, nearly all major animal phyla appeared in the fossil record—including armored trilobites, predatory Anomalocaris, and early chordate ancestors."

Tetrapod Colonization of Land

"Observing a muddy Devonian delta, I recorded transitional species like Tiktaalik using muscular lobed fins with wrist bones to haul themselves onto land, paving the way for terrestrial amphibians and amniotes."

💥 Mass Extinction Crisis #1 ~252 Ma

The Permian-Triassic Extinction ("The Great Dying")

Catastrophic volcanic eruptions in the Siberian Traps released massive volumes of carbon dioxide and sulfur, causing runaway global warming, ocean acidification, and marine anoxia. Over 95% of marine species and 70% of terrestrial vertebrate families were completely eradicated.

⚡ Evolutionary Cause-and-Effect #2

Flood Volcanism → Planetary Climate Collapse → Niche Reorganization: The catastrophic climate warming and oceanic dead zones wiped out dominant Paleozoic fauna like trilobites and sea scorpions, clearing open ecological niches for the survival and expansion of archosaurs in the Triassic.
